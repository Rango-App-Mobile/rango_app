# Marketing brief — Rango

Briefs are project docs: content in **Portuguese**.

## Steps

1. Read `docs/contexto.md` §1 (pain), §2 (benchmark) and §3 (differentiators),
   and what exists in `docs/marketing/` (`posicionamento.md`, `identidade.md`,
   `calendario-conteudo.md`).
2. If the brief is about a feature, read its spec in `docs/specs/` or
   `docs/<feature>.md`, so the brief never promises what is not in scope.
3. Next number: highest `NNN` in `docs/marketing/briefs/` + 1.
4. Copy `docs/marketing/briefs/_template.md` to
   `docs/marketing/briefs/NNN-slug.md` and fill it: one objective, one audience
   (a persona from `posicionamento.md`), one main message, a measurable success
   metric with a deadline.
5. Show it in chat and wait for adjustments.

## Limits

- Never promise a feature listed as future in `docs/contexto.md` §9.
- Never use a real restaurant's brand without permission.
- Dates tie to the course milestones in `docs/contexto.md` §10 (seminar 22/10,
  final presentation 03/12).
